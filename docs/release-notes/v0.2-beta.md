# Fractal v0.2-beta

The second public beta of **Fractal**, a Wii U emulator for Android handhelds and phones, forked from Cemu and tuned
for Adreno and Mali GPUs.

> **This is a beta.** Results still change from one device or GPU driver to another. Back up your saves and report what
> you find.

## Highlights

- **Performance HUD** in the look of your theme: FPS, frame time, CPU and GPU load, temperatures, RAM, battery and power
  draw. Pick what it shows, its size, opacity and layout, and drag it anywhere.
- **Graphic packs and cheats in the in-game menu**, applied to the running game (resolution packs still ask for a
  restart). The game's settings also open from the in-game menu, with a **Restart game** button.
- **Community shader caches:** download a game's shared cache from its settings and play with fewer stutters the first
  time; share yours anonymously, no account needed.
- **Choose where Fractal keeps its data** (internal storage or SD card), and install mods from a .zip or a folder.
- **Samsung Xclipse (Exynos) GPUs** can now use custom RADV drivers (not tested on an Exynos device yet), and faux123's
  PanVK joins the Mali drivers.
- **Faster on ARM:** hardware AES and CRC32 and new NEON code for disc reads, game loading, index buffers and texture
  decoding on Mali; the recompiler handles many more instructions.
- **Android only:** all the desktop code is gone (about 103 thousand lines), and the native library is 22.7 → 18.4 MB.
- **Mali fixes:** missing characters, black patches on normal maps and a render target crash fixed on the Mali driver;
  PanVK no longer closes every game on its first frame.
- Controller type per game, with a hint when a game ignores your setup; a lower resolution offered once when a game runs
  well below full speed; your own menu music and sounds; the debug report offered after a crash.

## Game fixes

**Super Smash Bros. for Wii U** (stage loading freeze), **Splinter Cell: Blacklist** (crash, now 30 fps instead of 20,
gets past the loading screen on the RP5), **Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate**, **Batman: Arkham City**, **Breath of the Wild**
with community shader caches, **New Super Mario Bros. U** on slower phones, **Resident Evil Revelations** and **Yoshi's
Woolly World** on Mali, **Pikmin 3** and the **Twilight Princess HD** minimap on the Adreno driver, **Sonic Lost World**,
**Rayman Legends**, **Paper Mario: Color Splash**, **Kirby and the Rainbow Curse**, **FIFA 13**, **LEGO Batman 2**,
**Teslagrad**, **Disney Infinity 2.0 and 3.0**, **Cars 3**, **Call of Duty: Ghosts**, **ZombiU** and **Hot Wheels:
World's Best Driver**.

Some games now come with a default profile (GPU driver, single-core) that avoids a crash or runs faster on most devices.

Full list with the commit behind each fix: [roadmap](https://github.com/dfdx047/Fractal/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md) ·
[changelog](https://github.com/dfdx047/Fractal/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Requirements

- Android 11 or newer, 64-bit ARM (arm64-v8a), a Vulkan 1.1 GPU.
- Adreno 6xx/7xx and Mali. Samsung Xclipse custom drivers are untested.
- Tested on Retroid Pocket 5 (Snapdragon 865), POCO X6 Pro (Dimensity 8300 Ultra, Mali-G615) and Xiaomi Mi A3
  (Snapdragon 665); earlier builds also on Anbernic RG Cube (Mali-G57), AYN Odin 2 Mini, AYN Odin 3 and AYN Thor.

## Install

Download the APK below, install it and follow the welcome setup. Fractal does not include or download games, keys or
firmware: use dumps of games you own. Decrypted games (.wua) need no keys.

## Known issues

- **Splinter Cell: Blacklist:** in missions only the sky and the ground are drawn.
- **Assassin's Creed III on Mali:** nearby terrain can be flat dark blue.
- **The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth (USA):** black picture (sound plays, no crash).
- **FIFA 13:** can crash in its audio thread on the RP5 (about 3 runs in 10).
- **Need for Speed: Most Wanted U:** a rare crash on the POCO (not seen in the last 7 runs); white foliage without the
  Bloom Fix pack (on by default).
- **Assassin's Creed IV:** the jungle can still turn black with only the HUD left (rare).
- **Watch Dogs:** Turnip can hang the GPU in long sessions (use the system driver); the picture can turn overexposed on
  Android; stuck compiling shaders on the RG Cube.
- **Ninja Gaiden 3 on Turnip:** dark floating heads and a blown-out sky in some scenes.
- **Xenoblade Chronicles X on the Qualcomm system driver:** white silhouettes; use Turnip.
- Custom Mali drivers (PanVK) are still experimental. Android has no immediate present mode: VSync off behaves like
  double buffering.

## Reporting bugs

After a crash Fractal offers to share its debug report on the next launch. You can also open **Options › Share debug
report** at any time. Attach `debug_report.zip` to a [new issue](https://github.com/dfdx047/Fractal/issues/new/choose)
with your device, GPU driver and the game's Title ID. Please do not report Fractal bugs to the Cemu team.
